**What Cena has.** Nothing built. The login `<settings>` blob, which is where Wrayth keeps server-side highlights, is consumed whole and dropped (`frame/vocabulary.rs:452-463` `Frame::ClientSettings`).

**The Wrayth highlight format, as these scripts read and write it** (highlight-manager.lic:3700-3770 and :2206-2233, stormsync.lic:797-825):

```text
<settings client='1.0.1.28'>
  <palette><i id='12' color='#33FF08'/> ...</palette>
  <strings local=''><h text='...' color='#RRGGBB|@N' bgcolor='' sound='file' line='y' case='y' word='y'/></strings>
  <names   local=''><h text='Name' color='...' bgcolor='...' case='y'/></names>
  <ignores><h text='...'/></ignores>
</settings>
```

Three kinds (ignore, string, name: stormsync.lic:728-738); flags `line` = whole line, `case` = ignore case, `word` = partial word (stormsync.lic:784-789, :815-824); colours are `#RRGGBB` or `@N` into a 110-entry palette whose defaults highlight-manager.lic:216-244 carries; attribute spellings vary by client (`foreColor`, `fgcolor`, `backColor`, `background`: highlight-manager.lic:5438-5439). The client pushes a new document with `<sentSettings/>`, and Wrayth answers upstream with `<db>` or `<stgupd>` (stormsync.lic:1136-1141).

**Presets in the pile** (the only shipped contents): profession fg/bg pairs, 10 current + 10 legacy (highlight-manager.lic:137-161); tag colours Friend/Family/Enemy/Self (:163-168); 13 race colours, current + legacy (:186-214); unknown-race colour `#7CFF00` (:300). Everything else in the pile is per-user.

### 3. LNet and other social or tracking protocols

**LNet (lnet2.lic)** is a separate chat network, not game protocol: TLS to `lnet.lichproject.org:7155` with a pinned CA (:74-110), XML elements `login(name, game, client, lich, password)`, `ping`/`pong`, `message(type=greeting|server|private|privateto|channel, from, to, channel)`, `query`, `request(type, to)`, `data(type, to)`, `tune`/`untune`, `moderate`, `admin`, `notify(type=new-spell-ranks|server-restart)` (:112-265, :596-640). **`data` bodies are base64 Ruby `Marshal.dump`** (:201-212) and are read with `Marshal.load` (:642): a non-Ruby client cannot speak them without a Marshal codec, and loading one from a network peer is an object-injection risk. Peers can request your `spells` (active spell -> time left), `skills` (all 46 ranks), `info` (race, profession, ten stats), `locate` (room title, description, exits, loot, PCs with status, NPCs), `health` (`XMLData.injuries`, four vitals), `bounty`, `mind`, `room` (map id) (:404-596), gated by an allow/friend/enemy list (:306-336, :1436-1470). It also uploads forage finds with the room's title/description/exits (:1206-1250). Chat is shown by injecting `<pushStream id="thoughts"/>[Channel]-Name: "msg"` (:267-300). **Cena has none of it**: `grep -rniE "lnet\|lichnet" crates plan research` = 0 files. Every datum a peer can ask for exists in Cena's model, so an LNet bridge would be protocol work only, with the Marshal problem the one hard part. Not on the roadmap; `plan/35` (M7) is the nearest neighbour (another process asking a session for its state). ignore.lic, xignore.lic, Live_Market.lic and resourcealias.lic drive LNet; 15lnet.lic untunes it for 15 minutes.

**Game-side social data the pile reads, and Cena's status:**
- ESP/thoughts channels (wizard_esp_tones, sesp, findme, voicemail, sortwho): GAP, see Captures. The speaker is a link (sesp.lic:2), so Cena's link model would give the `exist` id for free.

- `find <names>` and `befriend list` (friendList.lic:171, :361): GAP.
- `profile <name>` (plist.lic:66): the CONFLICT risk above.
- Logons/deaths by name (sdeath.lic:27-29, friendList.lic:279-294): GAP.
